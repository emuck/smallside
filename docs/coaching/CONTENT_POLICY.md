# Coaching content and provenance policy

## Purpose

SmallSide should publish useful original coaching material without copying protected plans, diagrams, videos or wording.

## Source hierarchy

1. Current national federation and league material
2. Recognized coach-education bodies
3. Peer-reviewed or established child-development guidance
4. Experienced coach contributions with field notes
5. Commercial or community material used only as inspiration unless licensed

## Required provenance

Every game record must state:

- source type and URL;
- source title and publisher;
- date reviewed;
- rights status;
- whether the SmallSide version is original, adapted with permission, or linked only;
- reviewer notes.

## Writing and diagrams

- Write instructions in original language.
- Create original diagrams using SmallSide visual conventions.
- Do not reproduce screenshots or source diagrams.
- Quote only when necessary and within applicable limits.
- Link to the controlling source for rules and policy.
- Translate underlying ideas, not protected expression.

## Coaching review

A game must be checked for age fit, safety, active participation, decision-making, realistic connection, explanation time, easier/harder adaptations and inclusive participation.

## Child data

Content and reflections must not contain surnames, contacts, medical details, rankings, diagnoses, talent projections or unnecessary individual profiles.
