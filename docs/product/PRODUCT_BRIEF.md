# Product brief

## Purpose

Give a volunteer youth-soccer coach a professional, tutorial-like season companion that works during planning, beside the field and during post-session reflection.

The first deployment serves a U7 boys team. The reusable product should support other young age groups and league formats without weakening child-first coaching principles.

## Product principles

1. Joy and belonging are primary outcomes.
2. Teach the coach, not only the activity.
3. Prefer a small library of repeatable games to constant novelty.
4. Maximize ball contact, decisions and play; minimize queues and lectures.
5. Make inclusion, safeguarding and player voice visible.
6. Separate verified rules and policy from coaching suggestions.
7. Keep reusable content source-traceable.
8. Collect no child-identifying data in the local-first core.
9. Keep the coach in control of adaptations.

## Coach modes

- **Plan:** rationale, equipment, setup, timing and adaptations
- **Pitch:** readable running order, cues and diagrams
- **Reflect:** lightweight anonymous observations
- **Learn:** short coach-development guidance with primary sources

## Current deployment context

This public repository always ships the fictional demo season (`data/seasons/2027-test-modularity-demo-u9/`) so no real team, coach or child identity is exposed here. A coach forking the repository fills in their own team, season and league profile via `tools/new-season.html` (see [CONTENT_PACKS.md](../architecture/CONTENT_PACKS.md)); those values belong in the active season and league profile, not application code or this document.

## Safety boundary

SmallSide is a planning and learning aid. Club safeguarding procedures, emergency plans, medical guidance and current competition rules remain controlling.
