# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

SmallSide exists to help volunteer coaches run better, safer, more inclusive
youth sport sessions. We hold contributors to that same standard: this is a
project about children's activities, and participation here should be
welcoming, respectful and safe for everyone.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences, especially from
  coaches working in different leagues, age groups and resource constraints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for coaches and the players they work with

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
- Publishing others' private information — including any child's identity,
  contact information, or performance/medical data — without explicit
  permission (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for this project's
  content boundaries)
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional or community setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by messaging the
repository maintainer directly through their GitHub profile, or by using
GitHub's own [reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
if contacting the maintainer directly isn't appropriate. All complaints will
be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response deemed necessary
and appropriate to the circumstances.
